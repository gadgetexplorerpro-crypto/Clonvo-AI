# Clonvo AI Account & Data Deletion Guide

**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026

Clonvo AI provides complete control over your voice data, custom voice clones, audio generations, and account information.

---

## How to Delete Your Account & All Associated Data

### Option 1: In-App Instant Self-Service Deletion (Recommended)
1. Open the **Clonvo AI** app on your device.
2. Navigate to the **Profile** tab (bottom right icon).
3. Scroll down to **Safety, Governance & Account Controls**.
4. Tap the red button labeled **"Delete Account & All Cloned Voices"**.
5. Confirm the deletion prompt.

**What gets deleted immediately:**
- Your user profile and credit balance in Firestore.
- All saved voice clones and custom speaker embeddings.
- All synthesized audio recordings stored on your device and in cloud storage.
- Your authentication credentials and active login session.

---

## Option 2: Data Deletion Request via Email or GitHub
If you no longer have access to the app or device, you can submit an explicit data deletion request:

- **Email Request:** Send an email to `gadgetexplorerpro@gmail.com` with the subject line `[DATA DELETION REQUEST] Clonvo AI`.
- **Information Required:**
  - Registered Gmail / Email address
  - Approximate date of account creation
- **Processing Time:** Data deletion requests submitted via email will be processed within 48 hours, and a written confirmation will be sent upon completion.
