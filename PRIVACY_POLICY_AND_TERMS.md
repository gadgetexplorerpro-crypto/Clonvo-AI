# Clonvo AI Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

**Last Updated:** October 28, 2026

Welcome to **Clonvo AI**, a professional AI voice cloning studio application (`com.aistudio.clonvoai.vxcqmr`). We take privacy, voice ownership, and transparent data practices seriously.

---

## 1. Privacy Policy

### 1.1 Voice Data & Audio Recordings
- **Voice Samples:** Audio samples recorded or uploaded for voice cloning are processed exclusively to extract acoustic voice embeddings and generate your custom voice profile.
- **Data Isolation:** Voice profiles and generated audio recordings are linked strictly to your authenticated account ID (Firebase UID or Studio Account ID). No other user can access, play, or clone your voice profiles.
- **Third-Party Processing:** Audio processing and voice synthesis are conducted securely via HTTPS using ElevenLabs API. Speech samples are never sold, rented, or repurposed for unauthorized model training.

### 1.2 Data Storage & Encryption
- **Local Storage:** Recorded samples and synthesized audio are stored in app-private storage on your device (`context.filesDir/voice_samples` and `context.filesDir/generated_audio`).
- **Cloud Storage:** User credits, voice profile metadata, and usage statistics are synchronized to encrypted cloud storage in Google Cloud Firestore.

### 1.3 User Rights & Data Deletion
- **Individual Deletion:** You can delete any individual voice profile or generated audio recording from your Library at any time.
- **Account & Data Purge:** You can request a complete, permanent deletion of your account and all associated voice data directly within the app under **Profile -> Delete Account & All Cloned Voices**, or via our [Data Deletion Request Page](https://github.com/clonvo-ai/privacy-and-terms/blob/main/DELETE_ACCOUNT_AND_DATA.md).

## 2. Terms of Service

### 2.1 Explicit Voice Owner Consent Requirement
- **Authorized Voice Cloning Only:** You may only clone your own voice or voices for which you possess explicit, legally binding, written/recorded consent.
- **Consent Verification:** Clonvo AI requires a mandatory recorded voice consent statement prior to finalizing any voice clone creation.

### 2.2 Prohibited Conduct & Misuse
- **Deceptive Impersonation:** Cloning voices of public figures, politicians, celebrities, or private individuals without consent is strictly prohibited.
- **Unlawful Activities:** Using synthesized speech for fraud, harassment, caller ID spoofing, extortion, or misrepresentation will result in immediate account termination, data deletion, and reporting to law enforcement authorities.

### 2.3 Mandatory AI Audio Labeling
- **Transparent Disclosure:** All exported audio files (MP3 and WAV) produced by Clonvo AI carry mandatory AI-generated disclosure metadata and visual watermarking labels.
